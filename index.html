How to scrapbook here

  • Photo — pick pictures (or drag & drop / paste them). Choose a frame, crop and filter on the right.
  • Write — add handwriting, sticky notes, tags, tickets or ink stamps. Double-click any writing to edit it.
  • Draw — doodle with pen, marker or pencil. The eraser removes whole strokes.
  • Collage — torn paper scraps, stamps, postmarks, tickets and pressed flowers to layer up. Paper → Start from a layout gives you a ready-made travel spread.
  • Tape & Stickers — decorate! Drag the corner handles to resize, the round handle on top to rotate.
  • Paper — change the paper of each page, or the cover material.
  • Use ◀ ▶ at the top to flip pages and + to add more pages.

Everything saves automatically in this browser. To keep it safe or move it to another device, use ☰ → Save backup file, then Open backup file on the other device.

Shortcuts: Delete · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z · Ctrl+D duplicate · arrows nudge · PageUp/PageDown flip
